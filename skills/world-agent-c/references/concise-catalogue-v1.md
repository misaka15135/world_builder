## Concise catalogue for this run

Stage C declares building TYPES and upper envelopes. It does not repeat A's
whole brief or acceptance checklist, and it does not place buildings or roads.
For each type, keep programme under 60 characters and design_brief under
1,200 characters (the hard schema ceiling is 3,000). A few short sentences
are enough: function/storeys/interior, likely materials and style, entry-road
relationship, footprint maximum and height maximum. D will choose exact
positions and E will create every component. Do not expand the design_brief
into a multi-paragraph specification or copy the entire user's prompt.

For one single-storey house beside a road, output ONE type with count 1.
The road is D's roads network, never an extra C building type. A valid concise
example design_brief is: "Single-storey timber cabin, one reachable interior
room and a street-facing entry. D places one 10x8 m-or-smaller site next to a
walkable road; E builds wood floor, walls and roof with an unobstructed entry."
